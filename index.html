Why we exist

Food is never just food.

Less waste. Affordable food. Dignity for everyone.

It’s the meal a mother prepares with love. The lunch a worker needs to get through a long day. The warm dinner a family hopes to share.

And sometimes, it’s the perfectly good food left behind when the day ends — still wholesome, still valuable, simply unsold.

We believe these two realities should meet. Not through pity. Through possibility.

One More Plate begins with a simple belief: when a community comes together, a little less food can go to waste, and a little more hope can find its way to someone’s table.

For a family

A safe, affordable meal can ease the weight of a difficult week — quietly, and with dignity.

For a food business

Surplus food can leave the kitchen with purpose, instead of becoming waste at the end of the day.

For a neighbourhood

Small, local actions quietly build a kinder, more caring Hubballi for everyone.

Two ways to give food a second chance

Two programmes. One shared belief.

One More Plate runs two distinct programmes. They share the same values but serve different purposes — and they never overlap.

Affordable surplus food

A. Good Food, For Less

Help people discover safe surplus food offered by participating food businesses at affordable, discounted prices.

  • Participating food businesses may offer safe, suitable surplus food at affordable, discounted prices.
  • Availability and collection conditions apply, and can vary from day to day.
  • This is an affordable-food programme. It is not free food, and it is separate from the donation programme.

Status: live listings, ordering and payments are not connected yet.

Community food donation

B. A Plate Shared With Love

A separate community food-donation programme connecting safe surplus food and voluntary donations with people who need them.

  • A distinct community food-donation programme, kept separate from Good Food, For Less.
  • Connects safe surplus food and voluntary donations with people who need them, through responsible NGO and community partnerships.
  • Donated food must always be free to recipients — it is never sold.

Status: distribution is planned and invitation-based while partnerships are being formed.

Partner with us

Please don’t confuse the two: Good Food, For Less offers surplus food at a discount for purchase. A Plate Shared With Love gives donated food free of charge. Donated food is never sold.

Simple steps, shared effort

How it works

A clear path for everyone who wants to help food find its way to a plate.

Food businesses

  1. 1 Register your interest.
  2. 2 Share details of suitable surplus food.
  3. 3 Follow safe handling and storage.
  4. 4 Arrange collection or discounted availability.

Customers

  1. 1 Look for participating businesses.
  2. 2 Choose suitable surplus food at a discount.
  3. 3 Collect during agreed times.
  4. 4 Enjoy your meal — and waste less.

NGO & community partners

  1. 1 Start a conversation with us.
  2. 2 Agree a responsible distribution approach.
  3. 3 Receive safe, suitable donated food.
  4. 4 Share it with dignity and care.

Volunteers

  1. 1 Express your interest.
  2. 2 Help spread the word.
  3. 3 Support safe handling where needed.
  4. 4 Be part of the movement.

These steps describe the planned model. Live listings, ordering, delivery and payments are not operational yet.

Safety and dignity, always

Food safety first

Every rescued meal must be safe to eat — and shared with dignity.

Safe sourcing

We focus only on suitable surplus food and voluntary donations — never anything unfit to eat.

Careful handling

Food is handled with clean hands, clean surfaces and clean, appropriate containers.

Correct storage

Storage time and temperature are respected so that food stays safe until it is shared.

Timely collection

Food is collected promptly within agreed windows to keep it fresh and safe.

Responsible distribution

Donated food reaches people with respect, care and discretion — free of charge.

Honest about status

We share verified details only, and never overstate what is in place.

In the interest of transparency: we are building our food-safety practices around responsible, widely accepted guidance for surplus food. We do not claim any certification, licence or guarantee that has not been independently verified, and we will publish verified details as they are confirmed.

Our local commitment
